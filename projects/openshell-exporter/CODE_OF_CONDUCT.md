# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, assume good intent while requiring
evidence, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.
